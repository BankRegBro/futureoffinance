About this Collection

Fiction as a lens for the near future of finance

These five stories are set in 2028 — close enough to feel inevitable, far enough to feel like warning. They explore the collision of technology and regulation: a corporate stablecoin that outgrows its issuer, a federal examiner that never goes home, a century-old trust doctrine quietly rewritten, a cryptographic failure no one believed possible, and a lending model that is almost right.

Each story stands alone. They share a world, and a question: when finance moves faster than the rules governing it, who decides what happens next?
