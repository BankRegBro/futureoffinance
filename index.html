How to read the file

The pieces are independent. Each carries its own reporting, its own interactive modules and its own disclaimer, and none depends on the others for its argument.

They do share a world. The charter question in 2027 sets up the deposit market of 2031, and the supervisory framework built to catch the failures of 2023 is the thing still running when the cryptography gives way in 2032. Read in sequence, the file is one argument about how fast the plumbing changes and how slowly the rules follow.

The Ledger · Forward file. Speculative pieces built from primary-source reporting, filed from a date that has not happened yet.

Every dispatch in this file is a work of narrative fiction. Each one identifies, in its own footer, which elements are drawn from the real record and which are extrapolation. Named institutions, individuals, figures and outcomes dated after 2026 are fictional constructs unless a piece says otherwise.
